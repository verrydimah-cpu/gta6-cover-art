**Vice Cover Studio** turns your photo and a caption into GTA-poster-style cover art: six angled multi-panel collage layouts, four colour grades (Vice Sunset, Neon Night, Golden Hour, Midnight Mint), bold Anton caption lettering with a neon glow, palm / sun / star / wave decor, a film-grain and Sharp finish, and one-click PNG export at 1080×1080, 1080×1350 and 1920×1080. It runs fully offline from static files — your photos are read on your own machine and never uploaded. Nine Surprise Me recipes remix the whole look in one click. Original design throughout; the Anton font ships under the SIL Open Font License (see `app/fonts/OFL.txt`).

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080 in a desktop browser, load a sample photo or your own, shape the caption, and export your cover. Tip: hit **Surprise Me** a few times — every recipe is a different poster.
